feat(MoE): Refactor cuda_graph_scope

Megatron-LMCUDA graphs

Turn `cuda_graph_scope` from a single string (`"full"` or `"attn"`) into a set of per-submodule scopes. A user names a combination of scopes — attention, dense feed-forward, MoE feed-forward, MoE router, MoE preprocess, Mamba, whole iteration — and for a given combination exactly the corresponding submodules of each layer end up inside the CUDA graph, while everything outside the combination keeps going through the ordinary eager pass. The hard part is the new case this unlocks: a MoE layer can now be captured only up to its router (and optionally through its preprocess step), and must then hand the tensors the graph produced over to the eager remainder instead of the remainder recomputing them.
