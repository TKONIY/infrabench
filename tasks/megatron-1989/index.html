[main] feat(moe): Support gated delta net for Qwen3-Next (1/4)

Megatron-LMModel support

Add a gated delta net (GDN) linear-attention layer to Megatron Core, and make a GPT model able to use it for some of its layers instead of softmax attention. Three pieces: (1) a new `megatron.core.ssm.gated_delta_net` module holding the layer itself plus a deterministic, torch-native implementation of its recurrence; (2) a decoder-block spec builder that assembles a heterogeneous transformer block — linear-attention layers and standard-attention layers interleaved on a configurable pattern, orthogonally combined with MoE and dense MLP layers; (3) the configuration, validation and command-line surface that drives it. This is the first of four PRs bringing up the Qwen3-Next architecture.
