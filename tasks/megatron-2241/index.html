Add muon and layerwise distributed optimizer

Megatron-LMOptimizer

Bring the Muon optimizer into Megatron, and add a layer-wise distributed optimizer to go with it. Two new modules under `megatron/core/optimizer/`: a tensor-parallel-aware Muon class plus the factory that builds a Megatron optimizer out of it, and a chained optimizer that splits optimizer state across data-parallel ranks by layer instead of replicating it on every rank. Both are selected from the training arguments (`--optimizer muon` and `--optimizer dist_muon`).
