Support multimodule pipelining in 1F1B schedule

Megatron-LMParallelism

Teach the non-interleaved 1F1B pipeline schedule to drive a graph of modules instead of a single model. A training job may now be composed of several separate modules — for example one or two encoders feeding a language model — each living on its own set of ranks with its own tensor/pipeline/data-parallel layout. The schedule must warm up, run steady state, and cool down over the whole composed pipeline, carrying per-module activations and gradients between stages.
