Add Maximal Update Parameterization (MuP) support

Megatron-LMOptimizer

Add Maximal Update Parameterization (MuP) to Megatron core. MuP is a parameterization in which a handful of quantities that are normally fixed constants — the standard deviation used to initialize weights, the attention softmax scale, a multiplier on the embedding output, a multiplier on the output logits, and the per-parameter-class learning rate (and Adam epsilon) — become explicit functions of how much wider the model is than a chosen base model. Concretely: the config learns a width multiplier, and every one of those quantities is derived from it at config construction time; the model applies the two multipliers at the two places they belong; the optimizer layer produces per-parameter-class overrides; and the training loop wires those overrides in.
