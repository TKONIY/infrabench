Non colocated refit

Megatron-LMWeight sync

Make weight refit — copying a trained model's parameters onto a differently-sharded inference model — work when the two models do not live on the same ranks. Today every rank in the job must hold both the source (training) model and the destination (inference) model. After this change a rank may hold only the source, only the destination, or neither, and the refit still completes correctly on every rank. Alongside that, three smaller fixes ride in the same change: the marking of tensor-parallel-replicated parameters, the way source data-parallel replicas are picked so the transfer load is spread evenly, and the quality of the communication schedule the NVSHMEM transport plans.
