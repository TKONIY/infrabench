MoE paged stash: sync-free expert execution + paged stashing of routed-expert activations

Megatron-LMMemoryMoE

Add an experimental MoE feature with two halves that ship together. Sync-free expert execution: when the user gives a per-expert-rank capacity factor, the HybridEP dispatch buffers and the fused grouped-expert buffers are pre-sized from that capacity instead of being re-queried and re-allocated every step, and a rank whose routed load exceeds the budget raises an over-budget flag. Paged stashing: the routed-expert activations that autograd has to keep alive for backward stop being one dense, capacity-padded allocation each and instead become page-granular tenants of a pre-sized stash buffer — a new `megatron.core.transformer.moe.paged_stash` module that owns the buffers, the pages, the free list, and the offload/reload of every stashed tensor. On top of both sits a runner that reruns a step once, without capacity padding and without paged stashing, if either the stash overflowed or a rank went over budget.
