[Megatron-FSDP] Add dtype customization to Megatron-FSDP.

Megatron-LMParallelismMemory

Give Megatron-FSDP a single, user-supplied mixed-precision policy object that carries three independently chosen `torch.dtype`s: the dtype of the main weight buffer, the dtype of the main gradient buffer, and the dtype used on the wire when gradients are reduced across the data-parallel group. Today those three things are controlled — indirectly and jointly — by two booleans, `preserve_fp32_weights` and `grad_reduce_in_fp32`, which can only say "FP32 or not". After this change the caller names each dtype explicitly, and Megatron-FSDP honours each one independently.
